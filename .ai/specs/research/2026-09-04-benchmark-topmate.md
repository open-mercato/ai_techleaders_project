# Benchmark — Topmate, checked 2026-09-04 by founder A

- Link: https://topmate.io/ and https://topmate.io/pricing
- What it does for our users' job, in one paragraph: a page for one expert to sell 1:1 sessions, priority DMs, webinars, courses and bundles "all from a single link"; discovery is the expert's own link and audience, not a marketplace. Closest to what the staff engineer we interviewed on 2026-09-03 asked for: "send them my link, don't put me in a list".
- What it does well: the expert owns the page, the price and the audience; "No CC required. No upfront fees. No recurring charges"; "Instant payouts" listed as a feature.
- Where it falls short for our users: any profession, not developer-specific; nothing about the expert's answers is kept or reusable; quality varies with the expert; a mentee with a blocker has no way to find the right expert unless they already follow them.
- Pricing or model, as shown on the date checked: commission 10% on sales through the expert's own profile or link, 20% per transaction for customers acquired through Topmate's marketplace; custom pricing for creators earning $20k+ a month. The expert sets prices; no ranges shown. (The board's revenue frame says 15 to 20% on sessions; Topmate's split by acquisition channel is worth a look.)
- Ratings: "100k+ reviews" claimed at the top; no formal rating system visible on the home page.
- Screens worth keeping as references: the single-link expert page as the pattern for a mentor share link.

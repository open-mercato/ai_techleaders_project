# Discovery board (Miro) — Competitors and benchmark. Exported 2026-09-02 by founder A (Oliwia)

Document type: the founders' own notes on competitors. No link and no date of checking were recorded on the board, so every line below is the founders' recollection until a benchmark-check note with a link and a date replaces it.

## Competitors (strengths and weaknesses, as noted by the founders)
- Codementor. Strengths: instant help by the minute, fast matching, large dev pool. Weaknesses: transactional, no continuity, knowledge lost after the call, $8–30 per 15 min feels expensive for small questions.
- MentorCruise. Strengths: vetted mentors (under 5% accepted), subscription model, free trial, 7,200+ mentors, strong reviews. Weaknesses: monthly commitment before you know fit, mentor chats only, no reusable knowledge, no tooling integration.
- ADPList. Strengths: free, huge community, strong brand with juniors. Weaknesses: free means no-shows and low mentor commitment, skewed to design and product, no monetisation for mentors.
- Topmate. Strengths: creator-led, mentor owns pricing and audience, async and 1:1 in one place. Weaknesses: generic (any profession), India-centric, no dev-specific tooling, quality varies widely.
- Preply. Strengths: best-in-class booking, scheduling and rescheduling UX, trial lessons, subscriptions. Weaknesses: languages only, model built on recurring lessons not urgent problems.
- Indirect: AI assistants — instant and free, but unverifiable and no accountability.
- Indirect: Stack Overflow / Discord / Slack — free, but slow, scattered, no guarantee of an answer.
- Gap noted by the founders: nobody sells mentor-owned knowledge as a product or delivers it inside the developer's tools.

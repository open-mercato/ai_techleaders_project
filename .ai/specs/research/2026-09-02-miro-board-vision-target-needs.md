# Discovery board (Miro) — Vision, Target Group, Needs. Exported 2026-09-02 by founder A (Oliwia)

Document type: the founders' own discovery board. Sticky notes are the founders' positions, not user statements; where a note restates an interview, the interview note is the source.

## Vision
- Motivation: the fastest learning in the industry happens in private conversations, and none of it survives.
- Change: turn those conversations into durable, paid, mentor-owned knowledge that developers can reach on demand.
- One sentence: mentors earn twice. Once for the hour, and again every time the knowledge from that hour helps someone else.

## Target Group (which market, who are customers and users)
- Market: paid 1:1 developer upskilling, between courses and bootcamps.
- Competitors named: Codementor, MentorCruise, ADPList, Topmate.
- Customer: working dev mid-transition to agentic engineering.
- Customer: freelancer / small-team lead with no senior to ask.
- User (mentee): concrete urgent problem, wants an answer not a course.
- User (mentor): senior engineer, income from expertise without consulting.
- Beachhead: English-speaking TypeScript, React, Python, AI-agent devs.

## Needs (which problem, what benefit)
- Problem: agentic engineering changes the job faster than courses or training can follow.
- Problem: the best answers live in private conversations and vanish when the call ends.
- Problem: expert help is either too slow (bootcamps) or too generic (courses).
- Problem: senior engineers can only earn from expertise by selling more hours.
- Benefit (mentee): a real expert's answer in minutes, hours or a session, priced by urgency.
- Benefit (mentee): keep pace with the shift without stopping work to study.
- Benefit (mentor): sessions become a product that earns every month.
- Benefit (both): knowledge from one session compounds instead of disappearing.

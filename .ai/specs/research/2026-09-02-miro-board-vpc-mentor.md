# Discovery board (Miro) — Value Proposition Canvas, Mentor. Exported 2026-09-02 by founder A (Oliwia)

Document type: the founders' canvas. Jobs, pains and gains are the founders' hypotheses about mentors; the one mentor interview (2026-08-22) is the only user statement behind them.

Customer segment: senior engineer / tech lead / OSS maintainer with expertise to sell.
Value proposition: turn your expertise into sessions and a vault that earn every month.

## Jobs
- Earn extra income from what I already know.
- Help developers without becoming a full-time consultant.
- Keep my own knowledge organised and current.
- Build reputation beyond my current employer.

## Pains
- Income stops when I stop taking calls.
- Answering the same question again and again.
- No-shows, reschedules, chasing payment.
- Platforms take a cut but bring no audience.
- Fear of my content being used without credit or control.

## Gains
- Recurring revenue without more hours.
- Predictable schedule, only the hours I publish.
- My name on everything, reputation compounds.
- Sessions produce reusable assets, not just billable time.
- Full control over price, scope and what gets published.

## Products and services
- Async Q&A via MCP with 24h SLA, priced by the mentor.
- Mentor-owned vault sold as a subscription.
- Booking, payment and scheduling for 25/50-min sessions.
- MCP endpoint so mentees ask the vault from their tools.

## Pain relievers
- Booking rules, prepayment and cancel policy handled by platform.
- Vault answers repeat questions so the mentor doesn't.
- Markdown in and out, no lock-in of your notes.
- Mentee approval flow, nothing published without both agreeing.

## Gain creators
- Three income tiers from one body of knowledge.
- Vault subscribers convert into session bookings.
- Dashboard shows what earns and what mentees ask for.
- Public profile with reviews, vault size and response time. (Conflicts with N02; the mentor interview rejected reviews.)

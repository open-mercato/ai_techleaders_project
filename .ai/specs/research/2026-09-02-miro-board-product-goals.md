# Discovery board (Miro) — Product, Business Goals. Exported 2026-09-02 by founder A (Oliwia)

Document type: the founders' own discovery board; positions, not evidence. Where a note conflicts with a later decision record, the decision record wins (D05 moves the vault to later).

## Product (what it is, what stands out, feasibility)
- Product: marketplace to find, book and pay senior engineers for 25/50-min sessions.
- Product: each mentor sells a knowledge vault as a subscription.
- Product: three tiers — ask the vault via MCP, ask the mentor async in 24h, book a session.
- Stands out: mentor-owned knowledge layer; competitors sell only hours.
- Stands out: answers inside your coding agent, not in a browser tab.
- Stands out: mentee approval before a session becomes public knowledge.
- Stands out: priced by urgency, not by seat; pay for speed, vault now, mentor in 24h, or live session.
- Feasible: marketplace flow is standard, proven by Preply and MentorCruise.
- Feasible: vault is markdown plus subscription gate, no new tech.
- Feasible: MCP is a thin read-only wrapper on the vault API.
- Feasible: MVP in 25 hours, one AI feature (answer from vault), transcription and AI note drafting deferred.

## Business Goals (how the product benefits the company)
- Benefit: recurring revenue from vault subscriptions, not just session commissions.
- Benefit: knowledge asset that grows with every session; competitors reset to zero each call.
- Benefit: mentors bring their own audience, low acquisition cost on both sides.
- Benefit: MCP access puts the product inside developers' daily tools, high retention.
- Goal: 100 mentors with published vaults in the first year.
- Goal: 30% of active mentees on a vault subscription.
- Goal: every mentor earns from at least two tiers.
- Goal: subscription revenue overtakes session commission by year two.
- Goal: prove the model in TS/React/Python/AI-agents before expanding stacks.

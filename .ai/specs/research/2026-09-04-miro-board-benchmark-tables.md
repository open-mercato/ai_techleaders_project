## Table 3. Benchmark summary (the founders' conclusions)

- Closest competitor in the domain: Codementor.
- Best marketplace UX: Preply.
- Best at building trust: MentorCruise.
- Simplest booking: ADPList and Topmate.
- Best tools for the mentor: Topmate.
- Biggest untapped opportunity: matching a mentor to a specific technical problem and its urgency.
- Direction for DevMentor: a problem-first marketplace for short technical consultations.

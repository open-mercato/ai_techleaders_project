# Benchmark — ADPList, checked 2026-09-04 by founder A

- Link: https://adplist.org/
- What it does for our users' job, in one paragraph: free 1:1 mentorship sessions booked in minutes ("The fastest way to get unstuck in your career"), with mentors from Google, Netflix, Figma, Amazon, Spotify, Microsoft; also mock interviews. Career help more than code help.
- What it does well: scale and speed of booking: "36,000+ experts", "160+ countries", "600M+ minutes of mentorship shared", "Trusted by 1M+ people"; booking "in minutes".
- Where it falls short for our users: free for mentees ("Free forever"), and the page says nothing about mentors earning anything, which is the mentor-side problem DevMentor exists for; dominant fields are design and product, with engineering present but not the centre; nothing about a session is kept.
- Pricing or model, as shown on the date checked: free for mentees; mentor compensation not stated on the pages read.
- Ratings: "Excellent 4.5 out of 5 on Trustpilot"; mentor profiles show star ratings (4.7, 4.4 seen) and session counts (a design mentor with 1,898 sessions, an engineering mentor with 866).
- Screens worth keeping as references: the mentor card with session count as a trust signal that is not a rating; worth discussing against N02.

# Source provenance and arithmetic corrections

This refresh uses materials supplied in the local DevMentor discovery fixture. Files describe their authors and collection methods; this session did not interview participants, access the underlying community or independently authenticate their accounts. Interview source tags describe those supplied accounts, not verified verbatim transcripts.

The fixture history includes editorial expansion of five interview notes into transcript form and later wording changes. It does not by itself prove that the underlying accounts were fabricated. Use the supported meaning of the supplied notes with attribution; do not repeat transcript details as independently witnessed events or exact verified quotations. Seven note files do not establish seven independently verified research participants.

The old `2026-08-25-support-export.csv` is explicitly identified as synthetic test data by `decisions/Q03-support-export.md`. It is excluded from observations about users, panel quotas and demand. The recovered panel based on it is historical material only and is not used by this refresh's new panel.

The newer Discord CSV and mentor CSV have supplied descriptions of source, period and filtering. The following are reproducible counts of those files, not an assertion that this session verified the external source systems:

- Discord: 109 threads dated 2026-08-18 to 2026-08-31; 23 with no human reply and 86 answered. The median first reply among answered threads is 67 minutes. There are 31 replies within 30 minutes, 39 within 60 minutes and 64 within 120 minutes.
- Discord resolution: 35 yes, 20 partial, 45 no and 9 unknown. There are 31 threads with an urgency signal, of which 9 have no reply. The supplied reading aid's 68-minute median and 23 partial resolutions are incorrect. Its claim that evenings are busiest is not supported by the per-hour counts.
- Mentor list: 50 candidates, all `not contacted`. There are 29 with a warm contact, 33 with public work found, 6 partly checked and 11 unchecked. This is a prospect list, not accepted invitations or active supply.
- Survey: 54 aggregate responses, with no row-level data supplied. Three selected having paid someone during the last blocker described by the question. That is not a lifetime count. Hypothetical price choices concern help within an hour, whereas the proposed product's earliest booking is two hours ahead. They do not validate its conversion or demand. The self-pay cross-tab in the reading aid cannot be reconstructed, and the two different counts for paying nothing are not silently reconciled.

Benchmarks are supplied records dated 2026-09-04. Their links and date are retained. This refresh does not present their prices or product features as freshly checked facts. A feature not mentioned on the inspected page is not evidence that the competitor lacks it everywhere. Miro comparison scores are team assessments without a supplied scoring rubric.

Any thematic comparison is with those supplied records, not a new real-user test. Synthetic results remain hypotheses regardless of overlap.

# Benchmark — Preply, checked 2026-09-04 by founder A

- Link: https://preply.com/en/how-it-works
- What it does for our users' job, in one paragraph: language tutoring, not code, checked only for the booking mechanics: trial lesson, then a weekly subscription with a tutor of your choice. The best booking and rescheduling flow we know, which is why it is here.
- What it does well: trial lessons are "25 or 50-min live lessons" (the same two lengths the workshop chose, by coincidence) and if the trial disappoints you can "switch to another tutor for free or opt for a full refund"; free cancellation "up to 12 hours before the scheduled time", after that full cost; unused hours: "1 week of your monthly hours will be carried forward".
- Where it falls short for our users: the whole model is recurring lessons ("subscriptions between 1 hour/week to 5 hours/week", billed "every 28 days"), the opposite of a one-off blocker; ratings and review counts are the trust signal on every tutor card, which N02 excludes for us.
- Pricing or model, as shown on the date checked: per-lesson rates on sample tutor cards PLN 137, 156, 186, 223; tutors set their own price (mechanism not described on the page).
- Ratings: every tutor card shows a star rating (4.89 to 5) and a review count (13 to 60).
- Screens worth keeping as references: the trial-then-subscribe flow and the 12-hour cancellation rule.

# D21 — In 1.0 a mentee finds a mentor through a list filtered by stack tag, or through the mentor's share link; no search and no ranking
- Date, owner: 2026-09-03, founder A (Oliwia). Recorded from the agent's recommendation on founder A's instruction to proceed with recommended answers; founder A confirms or supersedes it. Answers Q13.
- Context and the options weighed: the board's channel plan says mentors bring their own audience through share links; N02 forbids ratings; the beachhead is four stacks. Options: a search engine, a ranked list, a filtered list plus share links.
- Decision and why: a list of published mentors with a filter on stack tags (TypeScript, React, Python, AI agents) plus each mentor's share link. Order is by most recent published availability, never by a score (N02).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: more than 30 published mentors, or mentees reporting they cannot find a fit in the mentee interviews, reopens search for 1.1.
- Status: active

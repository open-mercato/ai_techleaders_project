# D26 — A session is live text with both present, starting at least two hours out
- Date, owner: 2026-09-07, Oliwia (founder A)
- Context and the options weighed: Mentors described two different things: answering between meetings ("tekst jest ok, mogę między spotkaniami. 25 minut to uczciwie, większość pytań to 10") versus a slot both people sit in. The fintech developer wanted a reply within twenty minutes, which is a wait rather than a session.
- Decision and why: A booked slot is a live text session with both people present for 25 or 50 minutes, starting at least two hours after booking. "An answer within a window" is async Q&A and stays Later.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: 1.0 deliberately does not serve the twenty-minute blocker; that developer is a 1.1 customer. The two-hour figure has no source in the material — it is a chosen number. A02 tracks the risk; a superseding row approved by Oliwia.

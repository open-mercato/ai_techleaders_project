# D15 — A vault answer always cites the mentor's name and the exact note
- Date, owner: 2026-09-07, Maciej (founder B)
- Context and the options weighed: The workshop of 2026-08-26 left this as its blocking open question. Mentors were unambiguous about attribution: "jak notatka z moim nazwiskiem i ta osoba się zgodziła, spoko. jak to idzie do jakiegoś AI, to chcę wiedzieć od razu", and the staff engineer called an AI answering from her notes "inna rozmowa" she wants to see before it ships.
- Decision and why: Every vault answer names the mentor and links the exact note it came from. Attribution is the mentors' condition for the vault existing at all, and with no ratings (N02) a citation is the only way a mentee can judge an answer.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Citation exposes note content to a subscriber who might then cancel, and makes coverage gaps visible. Closes the workshop's blocking question. Revisit by 2026-11-30; a superseding row approved by Maciej.
- Status: active

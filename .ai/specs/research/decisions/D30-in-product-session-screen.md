# D30 — Sessions happen in an in-product session screen; what "completed" means
- Date, owner: 2026-09-08, Maciej (founder B)
- Context and the options weighed: Where the exchange happens had been left open as non-blocking, but D06 counts completed sessions, D22 measures booking-to-start, and R09 and R18 both turn on who showed up. A channel the mentor names would make all three unobservable, and N01 had already promised a session screen.
- Decision and why: A 1.0 session happens in an in-product session screen — a text thread bound to the booked slot — and in no external channel. A session counts as completed when the mentee's question and at least one mentor reply exist in that thread within the slot; a no-show is the absence of one side's messages.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: The session screen becomes a 1.0 build item that the metric and both no-show rules depend on. Closes Q18. A superseding row approved by Maciej.

# D22 — Order by most recently published availability; instrument booking-to-start
- Date, owner: 2026-09-07, Oliwia (founder A); confirmed in the om-discover session
- Context and the options weighed: With no scores (N02) the list needs some order, and ordering by anything score-like would reintroduce what N02 excludes.
- Decision and why: The list is ordered by most recently published availability, never by a score. The product measures the time from booking to session start; a median above 48 hours in the first four weeks moves instant availability to the front of 1.1.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Rewards the behaviour the product needs without scoring anyone, and instruments the speed risk instead of guessing at it. Feeds D31's extension. A superseding row approved by Oliwia.
- Status: active

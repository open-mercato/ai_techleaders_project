# N03 — No mentorship programs or monthly plans; repeat bookings are welcome
- Date, owner: 2026-09-07, Maciej (founder B)
- Context and the options weighed: MentorCruise's monthly relationship is the model the staff engineer declined ("nie chcę relacji. chcę odpowiedzieć na pytanie i mieć spokój"), and the freelancer would return to the person who helped rather than to a plan ("napisalbym do tego co pomogl").
- Decision and why: DevMentor does not build mentorship programs or monthly plans. Repeat bookings with the same mentor are explicitly welcome — the exclusion is the subscription relationship, not returning to a person.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Writing it down is what stops 1.0 drifting into MentorCruise's model. Revisit by 2026-12-31; a superseding record by Maciej.
- Status: active

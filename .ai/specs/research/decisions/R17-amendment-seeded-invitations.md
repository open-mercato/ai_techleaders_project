# R17 (amendment) — No invitation goes out cold: warm intro plus a named developer with a question
- Date, owner: 2026-09-08, Oliwia (founder A) and Maciej (founder B), jointly
- Context and the options weighed: D18's check asks a mentor to publish a bookable session within two weeks of accepting. The one mentor asked what would make her publish answered "kto jeszcze jest. i pierwszy booking od kogoś, kogo znam" — a booking before publishing — and "to zapomnę, że to istnieje" after a silent month. As written, the check could have been unpassable by its own strongest source.
- Decision and why: Every invitation carries a warm intro from a founder and a named developer from the founders' network with a question ready, so a mentor can see a first booking before or just after publishing. D18's two-week check stands.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Makes A03's accepted-untested check passable, and puts a demand-side cost on every invitation. Revisit 2026-11-30; a superseding row approved by both founders.
- Status: active

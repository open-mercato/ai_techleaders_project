# D25 — 1.0 charges the developer's own card; no company billing
- Date, owner: 2026-09-07, Maciej (founder B)
- Context and the options weighed: Two customers were weighed. The own-card developer: the freelancer pays himself with a ceiling around 200 zł ("klient mi nie placi za odrabianie lekcji"), 24 of 54 survey respondents pay out of pocket. The expensing employee: 19 of 54 said an employer would pay (13 if asked, 6 without), but that needs invoices, purchase orders and company details.
- Decision and why: 1.0 charges the developer's own card through Stripe Checkout. No invoicing, purchase orders or company details. The interview price ceiling (200 zł, 50–100 EUR) is accepted as a constraint on what mentors can charge in 1.0.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Caps mentor earnings per session, and leaves 19 of 54 survey respondents unserved on evidence of n=1 against (from the developer already excluded as a customer). Revisit if the ceiling blocks mentor pricing under Q30; a superseding row approved by Maciej.

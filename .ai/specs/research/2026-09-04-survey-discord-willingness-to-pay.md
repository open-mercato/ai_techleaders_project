# Survey — "the last time you were stuck", posted on the developers' Discord 2026-08-29, closed 2026-09-03, run by founder B

Where: the same Discord as the help-request export (about 4,000 members), pinned in `#general` for five days. Anonymous form, no usernames stored. 54 responses. Nobody was told what DevMentor is; the form said "a short survey about getting unstuck".

Questions and answers, counts as exported:

1. Think of the last time you were stuck on something specific and had nobody to ask. How long ago was it?
   - this week: 11 · this month: 19 · a few months ago: 17 · can't remember one: 7

2. What did you do? (multiple choice)
   - searched / read docs: 49 · asked in a community: 31 · asked a friend privately: 26 · asked an AI assistant: 38 · shipped a workaround: 20 · paid someone: 3 · gave up on that approach: 9

3. If someone who had solved exactly that problem could have answered you within an hour, over text, what would you have paid?
   - nothing: 17 · up to 25 EUR: 14 · 25 to 50 EUR: 12 · 50 to 100 EUR: 8 · more than 100 EUR: 3

4. Who would have paid?
   - me, out of my own pocket: 24 · my employer, if I asked: 13 · my employer, without asking: 6 · my client: 2 · nobody, I would not pay: 9

5. Text or call, for that kind of problem?
   - text: 33 · call: 9 · depends: 12

6. How would you pick the person? (multiple choice)
   - what they have built or written: 41 · someone I know recommends them: 29 · ratings and reviews: 12 · price: 15 · response time: 27

7. What is your situation? (one)
   - employed at a company: 31 · freelance / contractor: 12 · student or between jobs: 8 · other: 3

Reading aid, founder B, 2026-09-04: 37 of 54 (69%) would pay something; 23 (43%) would pay 25 EUR or more; 3 have ever paid anyone. Ratings were the least chosen trust signal (12 of 54), behind response time (27) and public work (41). Employed respondents split between paying themselves (18 of 31) and asking the employer (10 of 31).

Limits: one community, self-selected, five days, a stated intention and not a payment; the "paid someone: 3" line is the only behaviour in the file. Read next to `2026-09-04-discord-help-requests.csv`, which counts what the same community actually did.

# E00-T03 — mentee interview protocol: would you pay for a named mentor's notes?

The study D03 reads on 2026-11-30. Copy this file per interview into `.ai/specs/research/` as
`YYYY-MM-DD-E00-T03-interview-{nn}.md` and fill it in.

- **Question the study answers:** would a developer pay for a named mentor's notes, as opposed to
  paying for the mentor's time? The vault, its subscription, MCP access and async Q&A return to
  scope only if at least 3 of 10 say yes (D03).
- **Run by:** Maciej (founder B).
- **Recruiting:** developers from the contacts of the 29 warm mentors in `2026-09-04-mentor-list.csv`,
  and survey respondents who said they would pay out of their own pocket
  (`2026-09-04-survey-discord-willingness-to-pay.md`, question 4).
- **Target:** 10 interviews. **Readout:** 2026-11-30.
- **Also carries** the frequency question that every earlier interview missed (A05, Q31) and the
  session-shape question behind A02.

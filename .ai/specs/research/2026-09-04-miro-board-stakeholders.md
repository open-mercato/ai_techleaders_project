# Discovery board (Miro), stakeholder matrix ("Raci Matrix" frame). Exported 2026-09-04 by founder A (Oliwia)

Document type: the founders' list of who has a stake in DevMentor, with each one's role and influence as the founders see it. On 2026-09-04 the frame titled "Raci Matrix" carried no responsible / accountable / consulted / informed assignment, only role and influence, so it is a stakeholder register; the RACI itself was added on 2026-09-05 and is in `2026-09-05-miro-board-raci-matrix.md`. Today the two founders cover every internal role themselves; mentors and developers are the users; external providers are Stripe for payments and whatever is chosen for email and calendar.

| Stakeholder | Role and interest in the project | Influence (founders' rating) |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor / product owner | Funds the project, sets its business goal, approves the scope | very high |
| Product Owner | Owns the product vision, priorities and the MVP scope | very high |
| Project Manager | Schedule, budget, communication and risk | high |
| Tech Lead / Solution Architect | Judges feasibility, defines the architecture and technical limits | high |
| UX/UI Designer | Runs discovery, designs the flows, the experience and the interface | high |
| Developers | Implement the solution and shape what is feasible | high |
| QA / testers | Verify flows, business rules and edge cases | medium or high |
| Mentors | Offer their knowledge, availability and paid sessions | high |
| Students / developers | Find mentors, book sessions and pay | high |
| Administrator / support | Handles the cases users cannot resolve themselves | medium |
| Marketing / Growth | Acquires mentors and students, communicates the product | medium |
| Finance | Controls payments, commissions, refunds and settlements | medium |
| Legal / compliance | Terms of service, privacy, data processing, marketplace rules | medium |
| External providers | Payments, email, calendar, messaging or video calls | low direct influence, but a dependency |

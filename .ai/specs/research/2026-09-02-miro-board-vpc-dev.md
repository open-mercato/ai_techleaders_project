# Discovery board (Miro) — Value Proposition Canvas, Dev. Exported 2026-09-02 by founder A (Oliwia)

Document type: the founders' canvas. Jobs, pains and gains are hypotheses about developers; the one mentee interview (2026-08-20) is the only user statement behind them.

Customer segment: working developer keeping pace with agentic engineering, no senior to ask.
Value proposition: a real expert's answer at the speed you need it, inside the tools you already use.

## Jobs
- Solve a concrete blocker before the deadline.
- Make an architecture or tooling decision I can defend.
- Stay employable as my stack changes.
- Learn the new way of working without stopping work to study.
- Get my PR or design reviewed by someone who's done it.

## Pains
- Courses are generic and outdated by the time I finish.
- Stack Overflow and AI give confident answers I can't verify.
- No senior around, or the senior is as new to this as I am.
- Hard to judge which expert is actually good.
- Booking a call for a 5-minute question is overkill.
- Knowledge from a paid call vanishes after the call.

## Gains
- Unblocked today, not next month.
- Answer from someone whose track record I can see.
- Pay only for the urgency I actually have.
- Keep what I learned, searchable, in my own tools.
- A mentor who knows my context over time.

## Products and services
- Search and book vetted mentors for 25/50-min sessions.
- Mentor vault subscription with searchable notes.
- Ask the vault from my coding agent via MCP.
- Leave a question via MCP, mentor replies within 24h.

## Pain relievers
- Ratings, reviews, vault size and response time visible before paying. (Conflicts with N02.)
- Three price points: ask now, reply in 24h, or live session.
- Answers cite the mentor's notes, no unverifiable guesses.
- Session notes I approved stay in the vault, free for me.
- Reschedule, cancel and messaging without support tickets.

## Gain creators
- Questions escalate automatically: vault to mentor to session.
- Follow specific mentors, build a relationship, not one-off calls.
- Everything reachable from terminal or IDE, no context switch.
- Vault answers most questions instantly, sessions for the hard ones.

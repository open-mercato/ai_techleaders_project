# Benchmark — MentorCruise, checked 2026-09-04 by founder A

- Link: https://mentorcruise.com/
- What it does for our users' job, in one paragraph: long-term mentorship by subscription: you pick a mentor, have a free intro call, then pay monthly for an ongoing relationship ("the same mentor month after month, not a fresh search-and-book every time"), with a cadence of every four weeks, every two, or a plan the mentor writes. Built for growth over months, not for a blocker on Friday.
- What it does well: a free intro call before any payment ("A free intro call, every time"); 7,200 mentors from named companies; the mentor relationship is the product and the copy is honest about it.
- Where it falls short for our users: monthly commitment is exactly what the staff engineer we talked to on 2026-09-03 refused ("I don't want a relationship, I want to answer a question and be done") and what the freelancer does not want to pay for; nothing about sessions is saved or reusable; the acceptance rate the board quotes (under 5%) is not on the pages we read today and stays unconfirmed.
- Pricing or model, as shown on the date checked: mentor rates displayed as "Starting from $100 /month", "$150 /month", "$350 /month"; "Prices don't go on sale". One-off sessions not visible on the home page. Refund policy not visible.
- Ratings: "97% of exit-survey ratings are 5 stars"; individual profiles show 5-star ratings. The mentor we interviewed on 2026-09-03 named this kind of rating as a reason not to join.
- Screens worth keeping as references: the mentor card (price from, cadence, intro call) as the pattern for a booking card without ratings.

## Decision summary

The requested backlog covers the full 1.0 session marketplace in D05. The historical 25-hour, fourteen-story cut in D03/D13 is an iteration inside that scope. It does not replace the complete MVP or justify generating only accounts and roles.

The supplied records describe developers who lost time searching for a specific answer and mentors who repeatedly answer unpaid questions. They also show people who solve problems internally and do not want another platform. The Discord export supports help-seeking in one community; the survey does not prove demand for DevMentor, its scheduled timing or its approved PLN prices. See Problems and A02–A04.

Now covers becoming bookable, discovery, booking and payment, the text session, private written output, notes and operator support. D27 records an in-app session; D28 supplies approved PLN bounds. Connect and payouts already belong to the full D04/D05 MVP. This seed retains that scope and its dependencies. Q19 concerns launching the shortened D13 iteration before its payout work is complete; the optional D29 proposal does not determine this full-MVP backlog.

The main mismatch remains urgency: D22 permits only slots at least two hours away, while some recorded situations need help inside an hour. Q20 leaves the non-booker research method undecided; Q21 leaves the ultimate marketplace stop threshold undecided. These questions block the named work, not every independent story. The independent review and current synthetic walkthrough are complete; H01–H18 remain hypotheses and the source corrections are recorded in `research/2026-09-14-brief-refresh.md`. The demonstration uses existing active decisions; it does not authorize launching the shortened D13 iteration with an incomplete payment chain.

## Key flows

- Current developer workaround: search, ask a colleague/community, then either obtain help, ship a workaround, delay or solve alone. Outcomes differ by supplied interview; no single combined story is presented as one participant's experience. `[INTERVIEW]` four mentee records cited in Problems.
- Current mentor workaround: reply selectively in DMs/Slack/Discord, often from a private document; three records describe different motivations. `[INTERVIEW]` three mentor records cited in Problems.
- Future mentor onboarding: operator invitation and role access → public-work profile and stack tags → approved-bound 25/50-minute PLN prices → published availability → mentor link/list exposure; Connect onboarding completes before the first payout under R05. The exact order relative to publishing the profile is a spec detail unless it changes an active product rule. `[DOCUMENT]` D04, D07–D09, D18, D21, D24, D28. Q19 remains a restriction on launching an incomplete iteration.
- Future booking and delivery: mentee finds a mentor → chooses duration and a slot at least two hours ahead → sees the price and rules → pays → receives booking confirmation → enters the DevMentor text session → receives the answer → receives a draft note → approves or declines/comment → keeps approved output privately. `[DOCUMENT]` D01, D02, D04, D05, D10, D20–D22, D27, D28.
- Future payment/support branch: unsuccessful payment must not represent a paid booking; cancellation uses R09; a quality dispute goes to the operator; platform fee and mentor payout use R05/R10 and are included in the full-MVP tree. Q19 blocks a shortened paid launch that omits this path, not drafting these existing requirements. Fine-grained payment states and failure handling belong in the covering spec; no nonexistent provider implementation is assumed. `[DOCUMENT]` D04, D10, D11, D19; `.ai/specs/backlog.md` identifies existing payment-related work.
- Future content boundary: booking confirmation and session screen carry R19; no public note or vault publication occurs in 1.0. `[DOCUMENT]` D02, D05, D26.
- Future measurement: count actual paid sessions, invitation publication and booking-to-start; Q20 must settle non-booker recruitment. Later vault testing remains separate under D14/D25. `[DOCUMENT]` D16–D18, D22, D25.

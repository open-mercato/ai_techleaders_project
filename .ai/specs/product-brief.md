## Product and how it stands out

- What 1.0 is: a marketplace where invited senior engineers become bookable by publishing slots and prices, developers find a mentor through a list filtered by stack tag or through the mentor's share link, book a slot that starts at least two hours later, pay for a 25- or 50-minute text session, receive a written answer, and approve or decline a note the mentor drafts, which both parties then keep privately. `[DOCUMENT]` `decisions/D05-scope.md`, `2026-08-26-workshop-export.md` (D01), `decisions/N01-no-video.md`, `decisions/D04-stripe-connect.md`, `decisions/D20-note-drafting.md`, `decisions/D21-finding-a-mentor.md`, `decisions/D22-session-timing.md`
- What the repository says the product is: the README's first paragraph still describes the whole vision (session marketplace plus a mentor-owned vault sold as a subscription and reachable from a coding agent through MCP); its scope note of 2026-09-03 says 1.0 is the session marketplace and that the vault returns to scope only after the E00-T03 readout, which closes Q16. `[PRODUCT]` `README.md`
- What comes later: publishing approved notes into a vault, vault subscriptions, single-note purchase, MCP access, team plans, async Q&A. `[DOCUMENT]` `decisions/D05-scope.md`
- What the founders intend to stand out on: a mentor-owned knowledge layer, answers inside the coding agent, mentee approval before a session becomes public knowledge, and pricing by urgency rather than by seat. `[DOCUMENT]` `2026-09-02-miro-board-product-goals.md`
- Of those four, only the mentee-approval rule (D02) and text-only 25/50-minute sessions (D01, N01) are in 1.0; the knowledge layer, the coding-agent access, and urgency pricing are later scope. `[DOCUMENT]` `decisions/D05-scope.md`, `2026-08-26-workshop-export.md`
- What 1.0 does not promise: no speed. A slot must start at least two hours after booking, 1.0 copy makes no promise of how soon an answer arrives, and the time from booking to session start is measured instead. `[DOCUMENT]` `decisions/D22-session-timing.md`
- Feasibility as the founders see it: the marketplace flow is standard, the vault is markdown behind a subscription gate, MCP is a thin read-only wrapper, and transcription plus AI note drafting are deferred. `[DOCUMENT]` `2026-09-02-miro-board-product-goals.md`
- The board's "competitors sell only hours" and "nobody sells mentor-owned knowledge as a product or delivers it inside the developer's tools" are the founders' recollection with no link or date behind them. `[ASSUMPTION]` `2026-09-02-miro-board-competitors-benchmark.md`
